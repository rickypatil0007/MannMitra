# 02 User Personas
# MANNMITRA — USER PERSONAS

---

# 1. PRIMARY PERSONA — STUDENT

## Role

College or university student.

## Core Needs

- manage academic pressure
- understand stress
- organize responsibilities
- feel supported
- connect with peers
- reflect on experiences
- access human support

## Common Situations

- exams
- deadlines
- project pressure
- loneliness
- comparison
- uncertainty
- academic failure
- transition into university
- difficulty asking for help

## Desired Experience

"I want help without feeling judged."

---

# 2. COUNSELLOR

## Role

Qualified human support provider.

## Needs

- receive appropriate support requests
- manage appointments
- understand relevant permitted context
- maintain professional boundaries

## Desired Experience

"I can support students without unnecessary exposure to their private
information."

---

# 3. FACULTY

## Role

Professor, mentor, or academic staff member.

## Needs

- understand broad student wellbeing trends
- identify areas where institutional support may be useful
- avoid accessing private student information without authorization

## Desired Experience

"I can understand where students may need institutional support
without invading individual privacy."

---

# 4. SENIOR / ALUMNI

## Role

Senior student or alumnus.

## Needs

- share experiences
- provide practical advice
- support younger students
- participate safely

## Desired Experience

"I can share what I learned without pretending to be a professional."

---

# 5. TRUSTED CONTACT

## Role

Person selected by the student.

## Needs

- receive appropriate communication when explicitly authorized
- understand why they were contacted
- support the student

## Desired Experience

"I know when and why I am being involved."

---

# 6. INSTITUTION ADMINISTRATOR

## Role

Authorized institutional administrator.

## Needs

- view aggregated insights
- understand broad trends
- identify opportunities for support programs
- maintain privacy boundaries

## Desired Experience

"I can improve institutional support without accessing students'
private lives."

---

# 7. PERSONA DESIGN PRINCIPLE

Do not design the product around a single "average student."

Students have different:

- backgrounds
- academic workloads
- communication preferences
- accessibility requirements
- comfort with AI
- comfort with sharing
- support networks

The system should personalize where useful while preserving control.
