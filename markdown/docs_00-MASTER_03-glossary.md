# 03 Glossary
# MANNMITRA — PRODUCT GLOSSARY

---

## MannMitra

The overall student mental wellness and support platform.

---

## Mitra

MannMitra's AI companion.

Mitra provides supportive conversation, planning, reflection,
organization, and contextual assistance.

Mitra is not a medical professional.

---

## Student

The primary user of MannMitra.

---

## Counsellor

A qualified human support provider connected to the platform.

---

## Faculty

A professor, mentor, or institutional staff member who may access
permitted institutional functionality.

---

## Senior / Alumni

A senior student or former student who contributes experiences,
stories, or peer support.

---

## Trusted Contact

A person selected by a student as a potential source of support.

---

## Institution

A university, college, or educational organization using MannMitra.

---

## Wellness

Non-diagnostic information and activities related to the student's
self-reported wellbeing.

---

## Stress Record

A historical record of a student's self-reported or permitted
stress information.

---

## Mood Record

A historical record of a student's self-reported mood information.

---

## Stress Forecast

A forward-looking estimate of potentially higher-pressure periods
based on available signals.

It is not a medical prediction.

---

## Wellness Analytics

Visualizations and summaries describing permitted wellness patterns.

---

## Reflection

A structured process that helps a student learn from an experience.

---

## Personal Note

A private student-created note.

---

## Voice Note

A private audio recording created by the student.

---

## Comfort Library

A collection of resources intended to provide calming, supportive,
or practical content.

---

## Quiet Space

A low-stimulation area of the product intended for calming,
reflection, or focused interaction.

---

## Community

The student-to-student social layer of MannMitra.

---

## Anonymous Identity

A community identity that allows participation without exposing
the student's real-world identity to ordinary community users.

---

## Peer Matching

A system that helps students discover potentially compatible peers
using permitted matching signals.

---

## Senior Story

An experience, lesson, or advice contribution from a senior or alumnus.

---

## Counselling Request

A student's request for human counselling or support.

---

## SOS

A safety-oriented workflow designed to help a student access
appropriate support during urgent situations.

---

## Aggregated Insight

Information combined across multiple users so that individual
students are not unnecessarily identified.

---

## Private Data

Information accessible only to the student and explicitly authorized
systems or people.

---

## Restricted Data

Highly sensitive information requiring additional authorization.

Examples may include:

- counselling information
- SOS information
- certain wellness information

---

## Public Data

Information intentionally made publicly visible within the applicable
platform context.

---

## Anonymous Data

Information that does not directly identify the student in the
displayed experience.

---

## Consent

Explicit permission provided by the student for an applicable
data use or sharing action.

---

## RLS

Row-Level Security.

A database-level mechanism used to restrict which records can be
read, created, modified, or deleted by different users.

---

## RBAC

Role-Based Access Control.

Authorization based on user roles and permissions.

---

## AI Orchestrator

The system responsible for determining which AI capability should
process a particular request.

---

## AI Context

Relevant permitted information provided to an AI system to improve
its response.

---

## AI Safety Layer

A set of controls designed to identify unsafe situations and prevent
inappropriate AI behavior.

---

## Product Loop

The MannMitra cycle:

UNDERSTAND
→ PLAN
→ PREVENT
→ EXPERIENCE
→ REFLECT
→ IMPROVE

---

## Light-First Design

A visual system where white and soft-neutral surfaces form the
majority of the interface.

---

## MannMitra Green

The primary brand accent used to communicate positive action,
progress, focus, and identity.

Green should be used as an accent rather than covering the entire UI.

---

## Glass Surface

A translucent interface surface using subtle transparency and blur.

Glass is used selectively and must not dominate the visual system.

---

## Primary CTA

The most important action on a screen.

Each major screen should normally have one clear primary CTA.

---

## Empty State

The interface shown when no content exists yet.

---

## Loading State

The interface shown while content or an operation is loading.

---

## Error State

The interface shown when an operation fails.

---

## Edge Case

A valid but unusual situation that must still be handled safely.

---

## MVP

Minimum Viable Product.

The smallest complete version of MannMitra that demonstrates
the core product loop and provides meaningful student value.
