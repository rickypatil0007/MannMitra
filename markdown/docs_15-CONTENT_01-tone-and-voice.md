# MANNMITRA — TONE AND VOICE

Status: Production Specification
Version: 1.0
Audience: Content Writers, Frontend Engineers
Platform: Responsive Web Application

---

# 1. PURPOSE

To ensure the entire application—not just the AI—speaks to the student in a way that is calming, supportive, and non-judgmental.

---

# 2. CORE VOICE

- **Warm but Professional:** We are a companion, not a best friend. 
- **Clear and Direct:** Stressed students cannot process complex paragraphs. Keep UI copy extremely brief.
- **Non-Clinical:** Avoid terms like "Severe," "Diagnosis," "Treatment," "Disorder."

---

# 3. UI COPY GUIDELINES

### Error States
- **Bad:** "Invalid Input." "Failed to save."
- **Good:** "We couldn't save that right now. Please try again in a moment."

### Empty States
- **Bad:** "You haven't done anything yet!" "No tasks found."
- **Good:** "Your schedule is clear. Enjoy the quiet." or "Take a moment to create your first plan."

### Loading States
- **Bad:** "Loading data..."
- **Good:** "Gathering your insights..." or simply a quiet, soft animation without text.

---

# 4. NOTIFICATION COPY

Notifications should never induce panic.
- **Bad:** "WARNING: 3 Assignments Due Tomorrow!"
- **Good:** "You have a few things coming up tomorrow. Let's make a plan."
