# MANNMITRA — FACULTY WELLNESS INSIGHTS

Document: `07-INSTITUTION/03-faculty-insights.md`
Status: Production Specification
Version: 1.0
Audience: Faculty, Backend Engineers
Platform: Desktop Web Application

---

# 1. PURPOSE

To provide professors with actionable, anonymized insights about the academic pressure their specific courses or departments are generating, enabling them to make empathetic scheduling decisions. (Supports Feature 18).

---

# 2. SCOPE

**In Scope (MVP):**
- Simple textual insights generated by AI based on aggregated data.
- Example: "A significant increase in academic stress has been detected among 3rd-year CS students around the current examination period."

**Out of Scope:**
- Predictive analysis of student grades based on stress.
- Connecting stress data to individual student attendance records.

---

# 3. GENERATION MECHANISM

1. **Trigger:** A weekly cron job.
2. **Data Assembly:** Pulls from `AggregatedInstitutionalMetrics` for the professor's specific department/cohort.
3. **LLM Evaluation:** 
   - *Prompt:* "You are an institutional wellness assistant. Look at these aggregated stress metrics. Provide 2-3 brief, objective insights for a professor. Do not make medical claims. Do not name individual students."
4. **Storage:** The insights are saved and presented on the Faculty Dashboard.

---

# 4. PRIVACY

- Faculty MUST NOT have access to the underlying raw rows used to generate the insights.
- The UI MUST display a clear disclaimer: "This data is aggregated and anonymized to protect student privacy."
