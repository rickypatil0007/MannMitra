# 03 User Journeys
# MANNMITRA — USER JOURNEYS

---

# JOURNEY 1 — NEW STUDENT

Landing
↓
Understand MannMitra
↓
Register
↓
Privacy Explanation
↓
Onboarding
↓
Personalization
↓
Dashboard
↓
First Wellness Check-in
↓
First Practical Recommendation

Goal:

The student understands the platform without feeling overwhelmed.

---

# JOURNEY 2 — DAILY CHECK-IN

Open MannMitra
↓
Dashboard
↓
Wellness Check
↓
Today's Priorities
↓
AI Recommendation
↓
Task / Wellness Action
↓
Continue Day

Goal:

Quick awareness without creating another burden.

---

# JOURNEY 3 — ACADEMIC PRESSURE

Upcoming Deadline
↓
Student Stress Increases
↓
MannMitra Detects Permitted Signals
↓
Stress Forecast / Awareness
↓
Mitra Suggests Practical Plan
↓
Student Adjusts Plan
↓
Tasks Distributed
↓
Reflection After Completion

Goal:

Turn overwhelming workload into manageable action.

---

# JOURNEY 4 — EMOTIONAL SUPPORT

Student Opens Mitra
↓
Shares Difficulty
↓
Mitra Responds Supportively
↓
Understands Intent
↓
Offers Practical Support
↓
Offers Human Support When Appropriate
↓
Student Chooses Next Step

Goal:

Support without diagnosis or forced escalation.

# JOURNEY 8 — REFLECTION

Important Event
↓
Reflection Prompt
↓
Student Records Experience
↓
Identify What Helped
↓
Identify What Was Difficult
↓
Identify Future Action
↓
Save Reflection

Goal:

---

# JOURNEY DESIGN RULE

Every journey should minimize:

- unnecessary steps
- unnecessary data collection
- repeated input
- confusing decisions

The student should always understand:

Where am I?
What is happening?
What can I do next?
